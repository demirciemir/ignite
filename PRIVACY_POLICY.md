# Privacy Policy for Ignite

**Effective Date: September 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how your data is handled when you use the Ignite application ("the App").

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Ignite is designed with your privacy in mind. We **do not** collect, store, share, or process any personal data. All of your timers, routines, and streak data are saved locally on your device using local storage. 

## 2. Analytics and Tracking
The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. Your usage remains 100% private.
